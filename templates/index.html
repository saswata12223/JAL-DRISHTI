🧠 Why this forecast?

JalDrishti combines rainfall, soil saturation, temporal trends and ESP32-CAM visual evidence. It also checks for an external-water pattern: if rain is absent but soil saturation keeps rising, flood risk can increase due to possible river overflow, dam release/break or another external water source.

Prototype note: the displayed flood probability and lead time are a fusion-model estimate. They must be trained and validated against historical flood events before being treated as an operational flood warning.
